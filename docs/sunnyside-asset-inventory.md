## Representation policy

- PNG is canonical for static art and PNG strips are canonical for animation.
- Equivalent GIFs, GameMaker `.yy` resources, pack-local Aseprite sources, and duplicate download payloads are intentionally excluded.
- Original source file paths are retained as the `source` property on every collection tile.
- The existing grid tilesets `sunnyside-world.tsj` and `sunnyside-forest.tsj` remain the canonical 16px world/terrain and 32px forest sheets, including their Wang sets.
- Animation timing and strip rectangles are centralized in `client/src/world/sunnyside-animation-manifest.ts`; v1.0 remains pending a real Aseprite-compatible CLI export.
